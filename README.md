# Rodel Oiga

I am a Creative Technology Program Manager (PMP, CSM) and
Principal at Lēdor Labs, based in Brooklyn, New York. Before
Lēdor, I spent 18 years at FIT and the School of Visual Arts
running technology operations for art and design programs: the
PrintFX and FabLab print operation at FIT, then systems for
the BFA Photography and MFA Interaction Design programs at SVA.

I manage delivery systems: the specifications, agent
skills, and verification rules that technical work runs on.
The engineering and the design belong to the people who do
them. I direct the work, and I review what engineers,
designers, and AI agents produce against the rules.

## What I manage

- AI-assisted delivery systems: structured ways for AI agents
  to do build work under written rules, with verification
  built in from the first step.
- Agent skills: portable instructions that give an agent a
  discipline, like checking its own work or gating a release,
  instead of a suggestion.
- Website and small-software delivery, taken from requirements
  through launch checks.

## Selected work

- [verification-loop](https://github.com/rodeloiga/verification-loop):
  a build-time verification loop for AI agents. The agent
  checks its own work, fixes what it is authorized to fix, and
  escalates the rest in one standard format.
- [preflight](https://github.com/rodeloiga/preflight):
  a pre-ship consolidation discipline. Every gate's findings
  gathered into one report, blockers presented as rulings,
  advisories listed with dispositions.
- [rodeloiga.com](https://rodeloiga.com): portfolio and case
  studies.

I improve the wheel: most of my work is taking a system that
already exists and making it work better for the people who
rely on it.

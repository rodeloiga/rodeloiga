# Rodel Oiga

I am a Creative Technology Program Manager (PMP, CSM) and Principal at Lēdor Labs, based in Brooklyn, New York. Before Lēdor, I spent 18 years at FIT and the School of Visual Arts running technology operations for art and design programs: the PrintFX and FabLab print operation at FIT, then systems for the BFA Photography and MFA Interaction Design programs at SVA.

I am not a software engineer or a trained designer, and this shelf does not claim to be engineering or design work. I direct technical programs: I write the specifications and the rules of verification, and I review what engineers, designers, and AI agents produce against them.

## What I build

- AI-assisted delivery systems: structured ways for AI agents to do build work under written rules, with verification built in rather than bolted on.
- Agent skills: portable instructions that give an agent a discipline, like checking its own work or gating a release, instead of a suggestion.
- Websites and small software, taken from requirements through launch checks.

## Selected work

- [verification-loop](https://github.com/rodeloiga/verification-loop): a build-time verification loop for AI agents. The agent checks its own work, fixes what it is authorized to fix, and escalates the rest in one standard format.
- [preflight](https://github.com/rodeloiga/preflight): a pre-ship consolidation discipline. Every gate's findings gathered into one report, blockers presented as rulings, advisories listed with dispositions.
- [rodeloiga.com](https://rodeloiga.com): portfolio and case studies.

I improve the wheel: most of my work is taking a system that already exists and making it work better for the people who rely on it.
